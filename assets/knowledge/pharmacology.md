# Pharmacology

## Pharmacokinetics and Pharmacodynamics
Bioavailability (F): fraction of drug reaching systemic circulation unchanged (IV = 100%). First-pass metabolism reduces oral bioavailability (e.g., nitroglycerin, propranolol, morphine).
Volume of distribution (Vd) = amount in body / plasma concentration. High Vd = extensive tissue binding (lipophilic drugs, e.g., chloroquine). Low Vd = stays in plasma (large/charged molecules, e.g., heparin, warfarin).
Clearance (CL) = rate of elimination / plasma concentration. Half-life t½ = 0.693 × Vd / CL. Steady state reached in ~4–5 half-lives (independent of dose). Loading dose = target Cp × Vd; maintenance dose = target Cp × CL × dosing interval.
Zero-order kinetics: constant amount eliminated per time (ethanol, phenytoin, aspirin at high dose — "PEA"). First-order: constant fraction eliminated.
Therapeutic index = TD50/ED50 (or LD50/ED50). Narrow TI drugs: warfarin, digoxin, lithium, theophylline, phenytoin, carbamazepine.
Agonist (full, partial), antagonist (competitive — surmountable, shifts dose-response right; noncompetitive — insurmountable, ↓Emax), inverse agonist. Potency (EC50 position) vs efficacy (max effect — clinically more important).
CYP450: CYP3A4 (most drugs), 2D6, 2C9, 2C19, 1A2. Inducers (mnemonic "Guinness, Coronas, and PBRs induce chronic alcoholism": Griseofulvin, Carbamazepine, Phenytoin, Barbiturates, Rifampin, St. John's wort, chronic alcohol). Inhibitors (mnemonic "SICKFACES.COM": Sodium valproate, Isoniazid, Cimetidine, Ketoconazole, Fluconazole, Alcohol (acute), Chloramphenicol, Sulfonamides, Ciprofloxacin, Omeprazole, Metronidazole, macrolides). Grapefruit juice inhibits intestinal CYP3A4.

## Autonomic Pharmacology
Cholinergic agonists: direct (bethanechol — urinary retention; pilocarpine — glaucoma, Sjögren; carbachol), indirect/AChE inhibitors (neostigmine, pyridostigmine — myasthenia gravis, ileus; physostigmine — crosses BBB, treats anticholinergic toxicity; donepezil, rivastigmine, galantamine — Alzheimer; edrophonium — historic MG diagnosis).
Anticholinesterase toxicity (organophosphates): DUMBELS (Diarrhea, Urination, Miosis, Bronchospasm/Bradycardia, Emesis, Lacrimation, Salivation). Treat: atropine (muscarinic) + pralidoxime (regenerates AChE if given early).
Antimuscarinics: atropine (bradycardia, organophosphate poisoning; toxicity: "hot as hare, dry as bone, red as beet, mad as hatter, blind as bat"), ipratropium/tiotropium (COPD — inhaled, no systemic effects), scopolamine (motion sickness), oxybutynin/tolterodine (overactive bladder), cyclopentolate/tropicamide (mydriasis).
Nicotinic: neuromuscular blockers — depolarizing succinylcholine (fasciculations then paralysis; contraindicated in burns/hyperkalemia/malignant hyperthermia susceptibility; phase I block not reversed by neostigmine), nondepolarizing (rocuronium, vecuronium, atracurium, tubocurarine — reversed by neostigmine or sugammadex for rocuronium).
Sympathomimetics:
- Direct α1: phenylephrine (decongestant, mydriatic, pressor), midodrine.
- Direct α2: clonidine, α-methyldopa (central ↓sympathetic outflow — hypertension, pregnancy-safe).
- Direct β1: dobutamine (acute HF).
- Direct β2: albuterol/salmeterol/formoterol (asthma), terbutaline (tocolysis).
- Mixed: epinephrine (anaphylaxis — α1+β1+β2; low dose β2 vasodilation, high dose α1), norepinephrine (septic shock pressor), dopamine (dose-dependent: D1 renal → β1 → α1), isoproterenol (β1+β2).
- Indirect: amphetamine, cocaine (blocks reuptake), ephedrine.
Adrenergic antagonists: α1 blockers (prazosin, tamsulosin — BPH, HTN; first-dose orthostatic hypotension), nonselective α (phenoxybenzamine irreversible — pheochromocytoma; phentolamine reversible), β blockers (propranolol nonselective; atenolol, metoprolol, esmolol β1-selective; carvedilol, labetalol α+β). β blocker overdose → glucagon. Never give β blocker alone in pheochromocytoma (unopposed α).

## Cardiovascular Pharmacology
Antihypertensives:
- Thiazides (HCTZ, chlorthalidone): DCT Na-Cl block; ↓Ca excretion (useful in Ca stones); adverse: hypoK, hypoNa, hyperGLUC (glucose, lipids, uric acid, calcium).
- Loop diuretics (furosemide, bumetanide, torsemide): NKCC2 in TAL; most potent; adverse: hypoK, hypoMg, hypoCa, ototoxicity, sulfa allergy. Ethacrynic acid = non-sulfa loop.
- K-sparing: spironolactone/eplerenone (aldosterone antagonists — HF, cirrhosis, PCOS), amiloride/triamterene (ENaC blockers); risk hyperkalemia, gynecomastia (spironolactone).
- ACE inhibitors (-pril): ↓angiotensin II, ↑bradykinin → dry cough, angioedema; teratogenic; protective in diabetic nephropathy, HF, post-MI.
- ARBs (-sartan): no cough/angioedema (no bradykinin effect).
- Direct renin inhibitor: aliskiren.
- CCBs: dihydropyridines (amlodipine, nifedipine — vasodilation, peripheral edema, reflex tachycardia), non-dihydropyridines (verapamil — cardiac > vascular, constipation; diltiazem — intermediate; avoid in HFrEF, combine cautiously with β blockers).
- Vasodilators: hydralazine (arterial; safe in pregnancy with β blocker; drug-induced lupus), minoxidil (hypertrichosis), nitroprusside (arterial + venous; cyanide/thiocyanate toxicity), nitroglycerin (venous > arterial; headache, tolerance needs nitrate-free interval).
Heart failure: mortality benefit — ACEi/ARB/ARNI (sacubitril/valsartan), β blockers (carvedilol, metoprolol succinate, bisoprolol), spironolactone/eplerenone, SGLT2 inhibitors, hydralazine+nitrate (esp. African Americans). Symptom only: digoxin, diuretics.
Digoxin: inhibits Na/K ATPase → ↑intracellular Ca → ↑contractility; vagomimetic → ↓AV conduction (AF rate control). Narrow TI; toxicity: GI upset, yellow-green vision, arrhythmias; hypoK potentiates. Treat: digoxin-specific Fab, correct K.
Antiarrhythmics (Vaughan-Williams):
- Class I (Na channel): IA (quinidine, procainamide, disopyramide — ↑APD, torsades risk; procainamide → lupus-like), IB (lidocaine, mexiletine — ↓APD, post-MI VT), IC (flecainide, propafenone — no effect on APD, contraindicated post-MI/structural disease).
- Class II: β blockers.
- Class III (K channel): amiodarone (most effective; pulmonary fibrosis, thyroid dysfunction, blue-gray skin, corneal deposits, hepatotoxicity — needs PFTs/TFTs/LFTs monitoring), sotalol, dofetilide, ibutilide (torsades).
- Class IV: verapamil, diltiazem.
- Others: adenosine (SVT — transient AV block), magnesium (torsades), atropine (bradycardia).
Anticoagulants: heparin/UFH (potentiates antithrombin; monitor aPTT; HIT risk — protamine reverses), LMWH (enoxaparin — predictable, renal clearance; partial protamine reversal), warfarin (vitamin K antagonist; monitor INR; teratogenic; skin necrosis early due to protein C depletion; reverse with vitamin K + FFP/PCC), DOACs (apixaban/rivaroxaban — Xa inhibitors; dabigatran — direct thrombin inhibitor, idarucizumab reverses).
Antiplatelets: aspirin (irreversible COX-1), clopidogrel/prasugrel/ticagrelor (P2Y12), dipyridamole, cilostazol (PDE3 — claudication), GP IIb/IIIa inhibitors (abciximab, eptifibatide — IV, ACS/PCI).
Lipid drugs: statins (HMG-CoA reductase; myopathy, hepatotoxicity; teratogenic; CYP3A4 interactions), ezetimibe (blocks NPC1L1), PCSK9 inhibitors (evolocumab — familial hypercholesterolemia), fibrates (PPARα — TG lowering; gallstones), niacin (↓TG, ↑HDL; flushing via prostaglandins — pre-treat with aspirin; hyperglycemia, hyperuricemia), bile acid resins (cholestyramine — ↑TG, GI upset, ↓absorption of other drugs).

## Antimicrobials
Cell wall synthesis inhibitors: penicillins (amoxicillin — otitis, strep, H. pylori; piperacillin-tazobactam — broad + Pseudomonas; nafcillin/oxacillin — MSSA; penicillin G — syphilis, strep, meningococcus), cephalosporins (1st gen cefazolin — surgical prophylaxis/skin; 2nd cefuroxime; 3rd ceftriaxone — meningitis, gonorrhea, CAP; ceftazidime — Pseudomonas; 4th cefepime — Pseudomonas; 5th ceftaroline — MRSA), carbapenems (meropenem — broadest, ESBLs; seizure risk with imipenem), monobactam (aztreonam — gram-negatives only, safe in penicillin allergy), vancomycin (MRSA, C. diff oral; red man syndrome — histamine, infuse slowly; nephro/ototoxicity), bacitracin.
Protein synthesis inhibitors: aminoglycosides (gentamicin — synergistic with β-lactams for endocarditis; nephro/ototoxicity, neuromuscular blockade; need O2 → no anaerobe coverage), tetracyclines (doxycycline — atypicals, Lyme, MRSA, acne; photosensitivity, teeth/bone deposition — avoid <8 yrs/pregnancy; chelated by Ca/Fe/antacids), macrolides (azithromycin — atypicals, pertussis, chlamydia; QT prolongation, CYP inhibition, GI motility), clindamycin (anaerobes above diaphragm, MRSA skin, toxoplasma; C. diff risk), linezolid (MRSA/VRE; serotonin syndrome with SSRIs, thrombocytopenia, optic neuritis), chloramphenicol (aplastic anemia, gray baby syndrome), metronidazole (anaerobes below diaphragm, C. diff, giardia, trichomonas, H. pylori; disulfiram-like reaction).
DNA/RNA: fluoroquinolones (ciprofloxacin — UTI, Pseudomonas; levofloxacin/moxifloxacin — respiratory; tendon rupture, QT, cartilage damage in kids, CYP inhibition), rifampin (TB, meningococcal prophylaxis; potent CYP inducer, orange body fluids), metronidazole (DNA damage in anaerobes/protozoa).
Folate pathway: TMP-SMX (UTIs, PCP pneumonia, MRSA; hyperkalemia, megaloblastic anemia, Stevens-Johnson; avoid 1st trimester and near term), dapsone (leprosy, PCP; hemolysis in G6PD).
Antifungals: azoles (fluconazole — Candida, crypto; itraconazole — dimorphic; voriconazole — aspergillus, visual disturbances; CYP inhibitors), amphotericin B (severe systemic; nephrotoxicity, infusion "shake and bake"), echinocandins (caspofungin — Candida, aspergillus; inhibit β-glucan), terbinafine (dermatophytes), nystatin (topical Candida), griseofulvin (dermatophytes; CYP inducer).
Antivirals: acyclovir/valacyclovir (HSV, VZV; crystalline nephropathy — hydrate), ganciclovir (CMV; myelosuppression), oseltamivir (influenza), tenofovir/emtricitabine (HIV/HBV), protease inhibitors (-navir), integrase inhibitors (-tegravir), NNRTIs (efavirenz — CNS effects), NRTIs (abacavir — HLA-B*5701 hypersensitivity; zidovudine — anemia).
Antimycobacterials: TB — RIPE (Rifampin, Isoniazid, Pyrazinamide, Ethambutol). Isoniazid: hepatotoxicity, peripheral neuropathy (give B6), drug-induced lupus, CYP inhibitor. Pyrazinamide: hyperuricemia, hepatotoxicity. Ethambutol: optic neuritis (red-green color blindness). Rifampin: CYP inducer, orange fluids.

## CNS Pharmacology
Antiepileptics: valproate (broad spectrum; hepatotoxicity, pancreatitis, teratogenic — neural tube defects, tremor, weight gain), carbamazepine (focal, trigeminal neuralgia; SJS in HLA-B*1502, aplastic anemia, SIADH, CYP inducer), phenytoin (zero-order kinetics; gingival hyperplasia, hirsutism, ataxia, teratogenic — fetal hydantoin syndrome, CYP inducer), lamotrigine (broad; SJS risk — titrate slowly), levetiracetam (few interactions, mood changes), ethosuximide (absence seizures — T-type Ca channels), benzodiazepines/barbiturates (status epilepticus first line), topiramate (weight loss, kidney stones, cognitive slowing), gabapentin/pregabalin (neuropathic pain).
Parkinson: levodopa + carbidopa (peripheral DOPA decarboxylase inhibitor), dopamine agonists (pramipexole, ropinirole), MAO-B inhibitors (selegiline), COMT inhibitors (entacapone), anticholinergics (benztropine — tremor-predominant young), amantadine. Antipsychotics can worsen Parkinson.
Antipsychotics: typical (haloperidol, chlorpromazine — D2 block; EPS: acute dystonia → benztropine/diphenhydramine, akathisia → β blocker, parkinsonism → anticholinergic, tardive dyskinesia → VMAT2 inhibitors valbenazine/deutetrabenazine; NMS: hyperthermia, rigidity, autonomic instability → dantrolene/bromocriptine), atypical (clozapine — treatment-resistant, agranulocytosis monitoring, seizures, metabolic; olanzapine — metabolic; risperidone — hyperprolactinemia; quetiapine — sedating; aripiprazole — D2 partial agonist, less metabolic).
Antidepressants: SSRIs (fluoxetine, sertraline, citalopram — first line; sexual dysfunction, GI, serotonin syndrome), SNRIs (venlafaxine — also pain, ↑BP), TCAs (amitriptyline — pain/migraine prophylaxis; anticholinergic, QT prolongation, lethal in overdose — sodium bicarbonate for QRS widening), MAOIs (selegiline, phenelzine — hypertensive crisis with tyramine, serotonin syndrome), bupropion (no sexual dysfunction, lowers seizure threshold — avoid in bulimia/seizures, smoking cessation), mirtazapine (sedation, weight gain), trazodone (insomnia, priapism).
Anxiolytics: benzodiazepines (GABA-A frequency ↑; flumazenil reverses; dependence), buspirone (5-HT1A partial agonist — GAD, no dependence, slow onset), barbiturates (GABA-A duration ↑; phenobarbital).
Opioids: morphine, fentanyl, oxycodone, methadone (long acting, QT), loperamide (diarrhea, no CNS), codeine. Effects: analgesia, euphoria, respiratory depression (μ), miosis, constipation, cough suppression. Overdose: triad of coma, pinpoint pupils, respiratory depression → naloxone. Tramadol: weak μ + SNRI (seizure/serotonin risk).
Migraine: acute — NSAIDs, triptans (5-HT1B/1D agonists; contraindicated in CAD), ergotamines; prophylaxis — β blockers, topiramate, valproate, amitriptyline, CGRP antagonists (-mab/-gepant).
Alzheimer: donepezil/rivastigmine/galantamine (AChE inhibitors), memantine (NMDA antagonist).

## Endocrine and Other Systems
Diabetes drugs: metformin (first line T2DM; ↓hepatic glucose; lactic acidosis risk — hold with contrast/renal failure; B12 deficiency), sulfonylureas (glipizide, glyburide — close K-ATP → insulin release; hypoglycemia, weight gain), meglitinides (repaglinide), TZDs (pioglitazone — PPARγ; fluid retention, HF, fractures, bladder cancer), DPP-4 inhibitors (-gliptin — weight neutral), GLP-1 agonists (-tide: semaglutide, liraglutide — weight loss, CV benefit; pancreatitis, medullary thyroid cancer risk in MEN2), SGLT2 inhibitors (-gliflozin — glycosuria; UTI/yeast, euglycemic DKA, Fournier; benefits in HF/CKD), insulin (rapid: lispro/aspart; short: regular; intermediate: NPH; long: glargine/detemir). DKA: fluids → insulin → K+ monitoring.
Thyroid: levothyroxine (T4, hypothyroidism), methimazole (blocks TPO; teratogenic 1st trimester — use PTU then), PTU (also blocks peripheral conversion; hepatotoxicity), propranolol (symptoms), radioactive iodine (contraindicated pregnancy), iodine (Wolff-Chaikoff).
Steroids: prednisone, dexamethasone; adverse — Cushingoid, hyperglycemia, osteoporosis, immunosuppression, adrenal suppression (taper slowly), cataracts, psychosis. Inhaled (fluticasone, budesonide) — oral candidiasis (rinse mouth).
Asthma: SABA (albuterol — rescue), ICS (controller — first line persistent), LABA (salmeterol — never without ICS), LTRA (montelukast — aspirin asthma, Churg-Strauss association), theophylline (narrow TI, CYP1A2), omalizumab (anti-IgE), muscarinic antagonists (ipratropium/tiotropium).
GI drugs: PPIs (omeprazole — irreversible H/K ATPase block; C. diff, hypoMg, B12 deficiency, osteoporosis with long use), H2 blockers (famotidine — CYP inhibition with cimetidine), antacids, misoprostol (PGE1 — NSAID ulcers; abortifacient), sucralfate, bismuth, antiemetics (ondansetron 5-HT3 — QT; metoclopramide D2 — EPS; promethazine), laxatives, 5-ASA (mesalamine — IBD), immunomodulators/biologics (azathioprine, infliximab — TB reactivation screen).
Rheumatology: NSAIDs (COX-1/2; GI ulcers, renal, CV; aspirin irreversible — Reye syndrome in kids), acetaminophen (hepatotoxicity — N-acetylcysteine), DMARDs: methotrexate (first line RA; folate antagonist — give folate, hepatotoxicity, myelosuppression, teratogenic), hydroxychloroquine (retinopathy — eye exams), sulfasalazine, leflunomide, biologics (TNF inhibitors — TB/HBV screen), colchicine (gout — microtubules; diarrhea), allopurinol/febuxostat (xanthine oxidase; allopurinol + azathioprine interaction, HLA-B*5801 SJS), probenecid, pegloticase.

## High-Yield Antidotes and Toxicology
- Acetaminophen → N-acetylcysteine
- Opioids → naloxone
- Benzodiazepines → flumazenil
- Organophosphates → atropine + pralidoxime
- β blockers → glucagon
- Digoxin → digoxin-Fab
- Warfarin → vitamin K + PCC/FFP
- Heparin → protamine
- Iron → deferoxamine
- Lead → dimercaprol, EDTA, succimer
- Methanol/ethylene glycol → fomepizole (or ethanol)
- CO → 100% O2, hyperbaric
- Cyanide → hydroxocobalamin, nitrites + thiosulfate
- TCA overdose → sodium bicarbonate
- Anticholinergic toxicity → physostigmine
- Malignant hyperthermia → dantrolene
- NMS → dantrolene, bromocriptine
- Serotonin syndrome → cyproheptadine
