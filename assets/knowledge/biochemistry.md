# Biochemistry

## Carbohydrate Metabolism
Glycolysis (cytoplasm): glucose → 2 pyruvate, net 2 ATP + 2 NADH. Rate-limiting enzyme: phosphofructokinase-1 (activated by AMP, fructose-2,6-bisphosphate; inhibited by ATP, citrate). Hexokinase (most tissues) vs glucokinase (liver/pancreas, high Km, induced by insulin). Irreversible steps: hexokinase, PFK-1, pyruvate kinase.
- Arsenic inhibits glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate dehydrogenase.
- Pyruvate kinase deficiency → hemolytic anemia.
- 2,3-BPG shunt (RBCs, via bisphosphoglycerate mutase) — no ATP produced.
Gluconeogenesis (liver, kidney): pyruvate → glucose; bypasses 3 irreversible glycolytic steps — pyruvate carboxylase (mitochondrial, biotin, activated by acetyl-CoA), PEPCK (GTP), fructose-1,6-bisphosphatase (rate-limiting), glucose-6-phosphatase. Substrates: lactate (Cori cycle), amino acids (alanine — glucose-alanine cycle), glycerol, propionyl-CoA. Cannot use even-chain fatty acids or leucine/lysine (purely ketogenic).
Glycogen: glycogenolysis — glycogen phosphorylase (rate-limiting; activated by phosphorylation via glucagon/epinephrine). Glycogenesis — glycogen synthase (activated by insulin, dephosphorylated). Debranching enzyme, branching enzyme.
- Glycogen storage diseases: Von Gierke (type I, glucose-6-phosphatase — severe fasting hypoglycemia, hepatomegaly, lactic acidosis, hyperlipidemia, hyperuricemia), Pompe (type II, lysosomal acid maltase — cardiomegaly, myopathy; "Pompe trashes the pump"), Cori (type III, debranching — milder Von Gierke + muscle), McArdle (type V, muscle phosphorylase — exercise cramps, myoglobinuria, second-wind phenomenon), Hers (type VI, liver phosphorylase).
Pentose phosphate pathway: G6PD rate-limiting → NADPH (fatty acid synthesis, glutathione reduction, respiratory burst) + ribose-5-phosphate (nucleotides). G6PD deficiency → hemolysis with oxidative stress (fava beans, sulfa drugs, primaquine, infection).
Fructose metabolism: fructokinase → fructose-1-P (aldolase B) → DHAP + glyceraldehyde. Essential fructosuria (fructokinase deficiency — benign), hereditary fructose intolerance (aldolase B deficiency — hypoglycemia, jaundice, cirrhosis after fruit/juice).
Galactose metabolism: galactokinase → galactose-1-P uridylyltransferase (GALT) → UDP-galactose. Galactokinase deficiency (cataracts only), classic galactosemia (GALT deficiency — cataracts, hepatosplenomegaly, intellectual disability, E. coli sepsis; stop galactose/lactose).
Pyruvate dehydrogenase: pyruvate → acetyl-CoA (cofactors: thiamine/B1, lipoic acid, CoA/B5, FAD/B2, NAD/B3 — mnemonic "Tender Loving Care For Nancy"). PDH deficiency → lactic acidosis, neurologic defects; treat ketogenic diet (lysine, leucine).

## TCA Cycle and Oxidative Phosphorylation
TCA cycle (mitochondrial matrix): acetyl-CoA + oxaloacetate → citrate → isocitrate → α-ketoglutarate → succinyl-CoA → succinate → fumarate → malate → OAA. Per acetyl-CoA: 3 NADH, 1 FADH2, 1 GTP, 2 CO2. Rate-limiting: isocitrate dehydrogenase.
- α-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase needs same 5 cofactors as PDH (B1 deficiency/beriberi/Wernicke in alcoholics impairs both → lactic acidosis).
- Fluoroacetate inhibits aconitase. Arsenite inhibits α-KG dehydrogenase.
- Anaplerotic: pyruvate carboxylase → OAA.
- Succinate dehydrogenase = complex II of ETC (FAD).
Oxidative phosphorylation: complexes I–IV pump H+ into intermembrane space; ATP synthase (complex V) uses gradient. NADH → ~2.5 ATP; FADH2 → ~1.5 ATP.
- Inhibitors: rotenone (complex I), antimycin A (complex III), cyanide/CO (complex IV), oligomycin (ATP synthase).
- Uncouplers: 2,4-DNP, aspirin overdose, thermogenin (brown fat) — dissipate gradient as heat, ↑O2 consumption, no ATP.
- CO poisoning: binds Hb (left shift) + cytochrome oxidase; treat 100% O2/hyperbaric.

## Lipid Metabolism
Fatty acid synthesis (cytoplasm): acetyl-CoA → malonyl-CoA (acetyl-CoA carboxylase, rate-limiting, biotin, activated by citrate/insulin) → palmitate (fatty acid synthase, NADPH). Citrate shuttle moves acetyl-CoA out of mitochondria.
β-oxidation (mitochondria): fatty acyl-CoA → carnitine shuttle (CPT-I rate-limiting, inhibited by malonyl-CoA) → cycles of oxidation/hydration/oxidation/thiolysis → acetyl-CoA + NADH + FADH2. Odd-chain → propionyl-CoA → methylmalonyl-CoA (biotin) → succinyl-CoA (B12 — methylmalonyl-CoA mutase).
- MCAD deficiency: most common FA oxidation defect; hypoketotic hypoglycemia with fasting; avoid fasting.
- Zellweger (peroxisome biogenesis), Refsum (phytanic acid), X-linked adrenoleukodystrophy (VLCFA).
Ketogenesis (liver mitochondria): acetyl-CoA → HMG-CoA → acetoacetate → β-hydroxybutyrate/acetone. Exported to brain/muscle during fasting. DKA: ↑ketones, anion gap acidosis, fruity breath (acetone).
Cholesterol synthesis: HMG-CoA reductase (rate-limiting; statins inhibit; induced by insulin, SREBP). Cholesterol → bile acids (liver, 7α-hydroxylase), steroid hormones, vitamin D.
Lipoproteins: chylomicrons (dietary TG; apoB-48; lymph → blood), VLDL (hepatic TG; apoB-100), LDL (cholesterol delivery; apoB-100; "bad"), HDL (reverse transport; apoA-I; LCAT; CETP; "good"). Lipoprotein lipase (capillary endothelium; apoC-II cofactor) clears TG from chylomicrons/VLDL.
- Familial hypercholesterolemia: LDL receptor defect (type IIa) — tendon xanthomas, early MI; homozygous severe. Type I (LPL or apoC-II deficiency — pancreatitis, eruptive xanthomas), type III (apoE defect — dysbetalipoproteinemia, palmar xanthomas), type IV (↑VLDL — TG).
Eicosanoids: arachidonic acid → prostaglandins/thromboxanes (COX-1/2) + leukotrienes (lipoxygenase). Aspirin irreversibly inhibits COX; NSAIDs reversible.

## Amino Acid and Urea Metabolism
Essential amino acids (mnemonic PVT TIM HALL): Phenylalanine, Valine, Threonine, Tryptophan, Isoleucine, Methionine, Histidine, Arginine, Leucine, Lysine.
Urea cycle (liver): NH3 + CO2 → carbamoyl phosphate (CPS-I, rate-limiting, N-acetylglutamate activator) → citrulline → argininosuccinate → arginine → urea + ornithine. One N from NH3, one from aspartate. Hyperammonemia: tremor, slurred speech, cerebral edema; treat lactulose, rifaximin.
- OTC deficiency: most common urea cycle defect, X-linked, ↑orotic acid, hyperammonemia.
Aminoacidopathies:
- PKU: phenylalanine hydroxylase deficiency (or BH4 cofactor); musty odor, eczema, fair skin, intellectual disability; treat ↓phenylalanine, ↑tyrosine (becomes essential); maternal PKU harms fetus.
- Maple syrup urine disease: branched-chain α-ketoacid dehydrogenase deficiency (I Love Vermont — Isoleucine, Leucine, Valine); sweet-smelling urine, ketoacidosis; treat thiamine.
- Homocystinuria: cystathionine synthase deficiency (marfanoid habitus, lens subluxation DOWN/inward, thrombosis, intellectual disability) vs Marfan (lens UP/outward); treat B6/B12/folate.
- Alkaptonuria: homogentisate oxidase deficiency — dark urine on standing, ochronosis (dark cartilage), arthritis.
- Albinism: tyrosinase deficiency (tyrosine → melanin).
- Cystinuria: defective renal/intestinal transport of COLA (Cystine, Ornithine, Lysine, Arginine) → cystine stones (hexagonal); treat alkalinization, acetazolamide.
- Hartnup: neutral amino acid transporter defect → pellagra-like (niacin deficiency).
One-carbon metabolism: folate → THF → N5,N10-methylene-THF (thymidylate synthesis) → N5-methyl-THF → methionine (B12, methionine synthase) → SAM (universal methyl donor). B12 deficiency traps folate as N5-methyl-THF ("methyl trap") → functional folate deficiency. Methotrexate/trimethoprim inhibit DHFR.

## Nucleotide Metabolism
Purines: synthesized de novo on PRPP (rate-limiting: glutamine-PRPP amidotransferase); IMP → AMP/GMP. Salvage: HGPRT (Lesch-Nyhan deficiency — hyperuricemia, gout, self-mutilation, dystonia, X-linked). Degradation → uric acid (xanthine oxidase; allopurinol/febuxostat inhibit).
- Tumor lysis → hyperuricemia; rasburicase (urate oxidase).
Pyrimidines: carbamoyl phosphate synthetase II (cytoplasm, no NAG activation) → orotate → UMP. Orotic aciduria (UMP synthase deficiency — megaloblastic anemia, no hyperammonemia; treat uridine).
Antimetabolites: methotrexate (DHFR), 5-FU (thymidylate synthase), 6-MP/azathioprine (purine synthesis; xanthine oxidase degrades — allopurinol interaction), hydroxyurea (ribonucleotide reductase), cytarabine/gemcitabine (pyrimidine analogs), fludarabine (purine analog), cladribine (hairy cell leukemia).
DNA repair defects: xeroderma pigmentosum (nucleotide excision repair — UV damage, skin cancers), Lynch (mismatch repair), BRCA (homologous recombination), ataxia-telangiectasia (ATM, NHEJ — cerebellar ataxia, telangiectasia, IgA deficiency, ↑AFP), Fanconi anemia (crosslink repair — pancytopenia, thumb defects), Bloom (helicase), Nijmegen breakage.

## Vitamins
Fat-soluble (A, D, E, K):
- A (retinol): vision (retinal), epithelial differentiation; deficiency — night blindness, xerophthalmia, Bitot spots; excess — hepatotoxicity, teratogenic, pseudotumor cerebri.
- D: Ca/PO4 homeostasis; deficiency — rickets (children), osteomalacia (adults).
- E: antioxidant; deficiency — hemolytic anemia, spinocerebellar degeneration (looks like B12 but no megaloblastic anemia).
- K: γ-carboxylation of II, VII, IX, X, C, S; newborns get IM shot (sterile gut); warfarin antagonist.
Water-soluble:
- B1 (thiamine): TPP cofactor (PDH, α-KGDH, transketolase, branched-chain ketoacid DH); deficiency — beriberi (dry: neuropathy; wet: HF), Wernicke (confusion, ophthalmoplegia, ataxia) → Korsakoff (confabulation, memory loss).
- B2 (riboflavin): FAD/FMN; deficiency — cheilosis, glossitis, corneal vascularization.
- B3 (niacin): NAD/NADP; deficiency — pellagra (diarrhea, dermatitis, dementia, death); excess — flushing.
- B5 (pantothenate): CoA; deficiency — dermatitis, adrenal insufficiency ("burning feet").
- B6 (pyridoxine): PLP — transamination, decarboxylation; deficiency — peripheral neuropathy (isoniazid), sideroblastic anemia; excess — sensory neuropathy.
- B7 (biotin): carboxylases; deficiency — dermatitis, alopecia, enteritis (raw egg whites/avidin).
- B9 (folate): one-carbon transfer; deficiency — megaloblastic anemia, neural tube defects (supplement in pregnancy), glossitis; no neuro symptoms.
- B12 (cobalamin): methionine synthase + methylmalonyl-CoA mutase; deficiency — megaloblastic anemia + subacute combined degeneration (dorsal columns + corticospinal), ↑methylmalonic acid; pernicious anemia (anti-intrinsic factor/parietal cell Ab).
- C (ascorbate): collagen hydroxylation, antioxidant, ↑iron absorption; deficiency — scurvy (bleeding gums, petechiae, poor wound healing, corkscrew hairs).

## Lysosomal Storage Diseases
Sphingolipidoses:
- Tay-Sachs: hexosaminidase A → GM2 ganglioside; cherry-red spot, startle reflex, NO hepatosplenomegaly; onion-skin lysosomes; Ashkenazi.
- Niemann-Pick: sphingomyelinase → sphingomyelin; cherry-red spot + hepatosplenomegaly; foam cells.
- Gaucher: glucocerebrosidase → glucocerebroside; MOST COMMON; hepatosplenomegaly, pancytopenia, bone crises, Gaucher cells (tissue paper); treat imiglucerase.
- Fabry: α-galactosidase A → ceramide trihexoside; X-linked; angiokeratomas, peripheral neuropathy, renal/cardiac failure.
- Krabbe: galactocerebrosidase → galactocerebroside; peripheral neuropathy, optic atrophy, globoid cells.
- Metachromatic leukodystrophy: arylsulfatase A → sulfatides; central + peripheral demyelination.
Others: Pompe (acid maltase — glycogen in lysosomes), I-cell disease (GlcNAc phosphotransferase — failure of mannose-6-phosphate tagging; coarse facies, clouded corneas, ↑plasma lysosomal enzymes), Hurler (α-L-iduronidase — gargoylism, airway obstruction, corneal clouding, hepatosplenomegaly) vs Hunter (iduronate sulfatase — X-linked, NO corneal clouding, aggressive behavior).

## Molecular Biology and Genetics
Central dogma: DNA → RNA → protein. Replication: helicase, primase, DNA pol III (elongation), pol I (primer removal), ligase; semiconservative, bidirectional from origins. Eukaryotic polymerases: α (priming), δ (lagging), ε (leading), β (repair), γ (mitochondrial). Telomerase (TERT + RNA template) extends 3' ends; active in stem cells/cancer.
Transcription: RNA pol II (mRNA; TATA box, transcription factors), pol I (rRNA), pol III (tRNA, 5S rRNA). Processing: 5' cap (7-methylguanosine), splicing (spliceosome, snRNPs; alternative splicing), 3' poly-A tail. α-amanitin inhibits pol II.
Translation: ribosome (40S + 60S = 80S), tRNA anticodons, initiation (Met-tRNAi), elongation, termination. Aminoglycosides target 30S; tetracyclines 30S; macrolides/chloramphenicol/clindamycin 50S (bacterial 70S = 30S+50S).
Mutations: silent (same AA), missense (different AA — sickle cell), nonsense (stop codon), frameshift (insertion/deletion not multiple of 3 — Tay-Sachs, Duchenne). Trinucleotide repeats: Huntington (CAG, anticipation, paternal), fragile X (CGG, maternal anticipation), myotonic dystrophy (CTG), Friedreich ataxia (GAA).
Genetic concepts: pleiotropy (one gene → many effects, Marfan), variable expressivity (NF1), incomplete penetrance (BRCA), anticipation (earlier onset each generation), imprinting (Prader-Willi = paternal deletion 15q; Angelman = maternal deletion — "Prader = Paternal deleted"), mitochondrial inheritance (maternal, heteroplasmy), Hardy-Weinberg (p² + 2pq + q² = 1).
Collagen: type I (bone, skin, tendon — most abundant), II (cartilage, vitreous), III (reticulin — blood vessels, granulation tissue; Ehlers-Danlos vascular type), IV (basement membrane — Goodpasture, Alport). Osteogenesis imperfecta (type I collagen — blue sclerae, fractures, hearing loss). Ehlers-Danlos (various collagen/lysyl hydroxylase defects — hyperextensible skin, joint hypermobility). Marfan (fibrillin-1 — tall, arachnodactyly, lens subluxation UP, aortic root dilation/dissection, MVP).
Scurvy = defective collagen hydroxylation (vitamin C).
